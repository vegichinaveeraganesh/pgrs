Division Governance Framework

How the PGRS Portal Accelerates Redressal

Seamless collaboration between citizens, Village Revenue Officers (VROs), Tahsildars, and the Revenue Divisional Officer.

Automatic Gist Preparation

As grievances arrive from the PGRS portal, the system summarizes the key subject, village, and relief requested into a standardized concise gist for immediate officer appraisal.

Mandal & Village Routing

Instantaneous mapping across Bheemunipatnam, Anandapuram, and Padmanabham mandals routes each case directly to the responsible VRO with contact details automatically linked.

Automated IVRS & SMS Dispatch

Citizens receive prompt SMS and IVRS automated phone notifications providing their assigned VRO name and contact number, eliminating uncertainty and repeated office visits.

SLA Escalation Monitoring

Strict 15-day resolution clock with near-breach warnings and beyond-SLA alerts automatically escalated to Tahsildars and RDO Bhimunipatnam.

Compliance Endorsements

Field officers upload inspection reports, patta proceedings, and enquiry endorsements. Grievance disposal is completely verifiable and transparent.

Citizen Feedback Loop

Closed-loop IVRS satisfaction rating (1 to 5 stars) captures citizen feedback and routes constructive ratings to divisional leadership for service improvement.
